## 🌟 About The Project

**What is Heaven Time?** is an interactive web page created to help kids explore the relationship between the physics of time, eternity, and the historical preservation of the Bible.

Through custom mathematical formulations, scriptural insights, and a manuscript timeline, this project breaks down complex concepts into engaging, digestible sections.

### Key Sections Included:

* **🌌 The Mystery of Time:** An intro connecting quantum physics, Einstein's theory of General Relativity, and how time functions in our universe.


* **⏳ God Beyond Human Time:** Scriptural breakdowns (2 Peter 3:8, Psalm 90:4, Isaiah 57:15) paired with a **Special Relativity Time Dilation Formula** calculating divine time.


* **✨ Eternity & Day/Night in Heaven:** Insights into Revelation 21–22 and Psalm 84:10 on what continuous light and God's presence mean beyond earthly timekeeping.


* **📜 Complete Biblical Manuscript Timeline:** An interactive timeline listing all **66 books** (Old & New Testaments) featuring:
* Author bios & background


* Estimated dates of writing


* Significant archaeological manuscript discoveries (e.g., Dead Sea Scrolls, Papyrus fragments)

## 🎨 Theme & Visual Style

Designed with a modern, dark-mode workspace layout inspired by *Meshy.ai*:

* **Deep Dark Theme:** `#0a0a0a` background with vibrant purple (`#8b5cf6`) and contrast blue (`#3b82f6`) glows.


* **Glassmorphism Cards:** Translucent backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter`) for content panels.


* **Typography:** Clean, readable text powered by Google's [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) font.


* **Interactive Elements:** Smooth timeline navigation and dynamic rendering via vanilla JavaScript.
